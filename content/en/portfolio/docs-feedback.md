As a technical writer, I kept running into a familiar challenge: the team rarely had a real-time, data-backed view of where users actually got stuck. A traditional 'write-and-publish' approach often left documentation out of sync with real user friction — we were writing what we assumed readers needed, rather than addressing what they were actively searching for.

To bridge this gap, I led a data-driven improvement loop powered by AI Q&A logs and user feedback metrics. By feeding quantified user signals back into our content architecture, we reduced troubleshooting friction for developers and lowered the ongoing workload for our support team.

---

## Method: A Data → Fix → Verify Loop

The iteration cycle centered on three core phases:

#### 1. Data Collection & Processing
Using our Mintlify docs backend, I exported raw conversation logs generated by the built-in Ask AI assistant across our Product Manuals and Developer Hub. To process this large, fragmented dataset, we built an AI-assisted workflow to clean, cluster, and aggregate high-frequency queries — stripping away noise to surface genuine user friction points.

Sample query distribution from a single log analysis cycle:

| Product Area | Mentions | Share | Interaction Pattern |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MPC Wallets** | 211 | 25% | Conceptual / Task-based |
| **Risk Controls** | 115 | 14% | UI / Configuration |
| **Custodial Wallets** | 109 | 13% | Conceptual / Task-based |
| **Developer Console** | 90 | 11% | UI / Configuration |
| **Address Screening & KYT** | 85 | 10% | Functional Workflow |

#### 2. Multi-Dimensional Attribution
Once cleaned, I categorized high-frequency queries into distinct buckets: genuine documentation gaps (Doc Gaps), AI retrieval/RAG mismatch (AI Strategy Issues), or underlying product/API behavior constraints. This root-cause attribution prevented us from modifying the wrong layer.

#### 3. Targeted Refactoring & Impact Measurement
For each identified pain point, we shipped targeted documentation fixes (e.g., dedicated troubleshooting guides, clearer diagramming, or comprehensive API error-code mapping). We then tracked the next cycle’s metrics to confirm higher AI answer success rates and fewer unanswered queries.

---

## What We Changed in Practice

Analyzing several months of AI Q&A logs led to concrete, high-impact refinements:

#### Rebuilding Content Around Top User Friction
- **Addressing critical information gaps:** For our #1 user query regarding transaction and payment processing statuses, we added comprehensive status-definition reference tables and placed a prominent 'Transaction Delay Troubleshooting Guide' right on the entry page.
- **Lowering cognitive load with practical guides:** For complex fee structures and recurring integration errors, we replaced fragmented prose with intuitive fund-flow diagrams and error-to-resolution mapping tables.
- **Decoupling complex workflows by user intent:** For urgent queries around authenticator lockout (Cobo Guard), we split a single bloated article into two scenario-based guides: 'Migrating to a New Phone' vs. 'Resetting a Lost Device.'

#### Making Content AI-Ingestible (RAG Optimization)
In the LLM era, technical documentation must be structured for machine readability as well as human comprehension:

- **Standardized terminology and metadata:** Unified field naming and technical terms across all pages to eliminate synonym drift in vector embeddings, and enriched frontmatter metadata for cleaner indexing.
- **Semantic structure for RAG retrieval:** Carefully refactored page titles, section headings (H2/H3), and Table of Contents (TOC) structures to align with natural-language user queries, significantly improving semantic retrieval accuracy and RAG context recall.

---

## Results & Business Value

- **Measurable Quality Lift:** Through structured sprint cycles and log reviews, overall AI Q&A answer accuracy maintained a high success rate (90%–95%+), while unanswered queries dropped substantially.
- **Lower Support Costs:** Converting top integration blockers into structured FAQs and self-serve troubleshooting guides reduced friction for onboarding B2B clients and alleviated support ticket volume.
- **A Sustainable Operating Rhythm:** We established a recurring weekly/biweekly log review cadence, transforming technical documentation from a static asset into a self-evolving product experience.
